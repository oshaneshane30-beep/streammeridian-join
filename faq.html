Early Signup FAQ

I already have cable or streaming. Do I need to cancel it?

No. Keep what you already use. Joining Meridian simply places your request in the readiness journey so you can decide later.

Do I pay now?

No payment is required merely to join the readiness queue.

Does signup activate service?

No. Meridian contacts you when activation is available for your account and confirms the supported setup first.

Which exact channels are included?

Package categories are shown during early signup. Final channel/category availability is confirmed before activation because provider availability can change.

Can I watch in a web browser?

Meridian is qualifying secure browser playback. Do not rely on browser playback until Meridian confirms it for your account. A compatible player/app option may be used where needed.

What devices may be supported?

Meridian is preparing options across common TVs, phones/tablets, TV devices and computers. The exact supported setup is confirmed before activation.

What if my account is not ready immediately?

Your request remains in the service-readiness queue. You do not need to submit another request.

Can a freelancer take my password or card information?

No. Do not give a referral contact passwords, OTPs, PINs, card details, banking credentials or provider credentials.

How do I get help?

Email support@streammeridian.com.
