Readiness Queue Terms

Effective 4 October 2026

No payment or activation from signup

Joining the Meridian readiness queue does not by itself activate service, create a customer-money charge, guarantee an activation date or require you to cancel an existing entertainment service.

Package information

Early package names, pricing concepts, channel/category availability, device support and playback methods may change before activation. Meridian confirms the supported package and current commercial terms before payment or activation.

Accurate information

Please provide accurate contact and device information so Meridian can manage your request. Do not create duplicate or false signups, and do not share another person's information without permission.

Referral attribution

A referral code records attribution only. A signup alone does not create a commission, payment or entitlement. Any freelancer or referral reward is governed separately by the applicable Meridian programme rules.

Communications

Operational messages may be sent to manage a signup, readiness state, activation or support request. Optional marketing is separate. You may ask to stop optional marketing without cancelling your readiness request.

Availability and responsible use

Activation can depend on provider, account, device, technical, security, geographic or service-readiness requirements. Meridian may pause or refuse activation where those requirements are not satisfied. Customer-facing communications will not disclose unnecessary internal security or network details.

Contact

Questions: support@streammeridian.com. Never email passwords, OTPs, PINs, card data or banking credentials.
